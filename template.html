Understanding how language models

I’m a Ph.D. student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). My research focuses on mechanistic interpretability, continual learning, and agentic systems.

My work explores how language models retain knowledge and reason across cultures and domains, with applications in Arabic NLP and financial AI.
