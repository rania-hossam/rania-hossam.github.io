About me

I am a Ph.D. student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). My research focuses on mechanistic interpretability, continual learning, and agentic systems. I study how language models retain knowledge, update their representations, and reason across cultures and domains.

I completed my M.S. in Natural Language Processing at MBZUAI, where my thesis was supervised by Prof. Preslav Nakov. My work spans Arabic NLP and financial AI, including domain adaptation, cultural reasoning, and the evaluation of language models.
